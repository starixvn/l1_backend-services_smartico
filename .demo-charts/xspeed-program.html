Segmented Tournaments

We separate "Luck" from "Skill" to ensure fair competition. Slot players chase multipliers (Track A), while VIP Table players compete on volume (Track B).

Tournament Profile Comparison

Visualizing the difference between Slot Sprints (Mass Market) and Table Wars (VIP/High Roller).

Track A: Slot Sprint

  • Mechanic: Highest Win Multiplier (e.g., 100x).
  • Audience: Low & High Budget players equalized.
  • Risk Control: Flag score jumps >500% in 1 minute.

Track B: Table Wars

  • Mechanic: Total Turnover/Wager.
  • Audience: Blackjack & Baccarat VIPs.
  • Risk Control: Exclude risk-free bets (Red+Black).
